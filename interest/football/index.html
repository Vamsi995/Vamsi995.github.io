Football — FAQ

QuestionAnswer
What is a high press?Defending aggressively in the opponent’s third to force errors.
Why use a 4‑3‑3?Balances width, midfield triangles, and pressing triggers.
What is xG?Expected goals; the quality of chances based on shot features.
What is a low block?Compact defensive shape near your box, denying central space.
Inverted fullbacks?FBs step into midfield to create overloads and protect transitions.
Counter‑pressing?Immediate press after losing the ball to regain possession quickly.
Set‑piece value?High‑leverage chances from rehearsed routines and matchups.
Over/underloads?Numerical advantages on one flank to isolate or combine.
Why rotate squads?Manage fatigue, injuries, and maintain high-intensity pressing.
Key metrics?PPDA, field tilt, progressive passes, counter‑press regains.
