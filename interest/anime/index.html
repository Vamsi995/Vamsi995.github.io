Why I Enjoy Anime

Anime mixes striking visual design with character‑driven arcs—great for exploring themes across sci‑fi, fantasy, and slice‑of‑life genres.

Anime — FAQ

QuestionAnswer
What is a cour?Roughly a 12–13 episode season block.
Sub vs dub?Preference; subs keep original delivery, dubs aid accessibility.
Popular genres?Shonen, seinen, shojo, slice‑of‑life, mecha, isekai.
What is sakuga?Standout animation cuts with extra detail and motion.
Why seasonal shows?Studios align releases with quarterly TV/streaming schedules.
Manga vs anime?Manga is the source for many series; adaptations can diverge.
Movie vs series?Movies offer higher production values; series allow longer arcs.
Starter picks?Try a genre sampler: adventure, sports, slice‑of‑life, sci‑fi.
What is OST?Original soundtrack—themes that define a show’s mood.
Community?Conventions, fan art, AMVs, and discussion boards build fandoms.
