Why I Enjoy League of Legends

LoL blends strategic macro decisions with fast mechanical play. Drafting, vision control, and objective timing reward planning and teamwork.

League of Legends — FAQ

QuestionAnswer
What is MMR?Hidden rating aligning you with similarly skilled players.
Why ward?Vision prevents ganks, secures objectives, and enables map control.
When to take Dragon?After bot prio or enemy recall/death; track smite timers.
Split push vs group?Split with TP and deep vision; group when winning fights or for soul.
What is tempo?The initiative gained by acting while the opponent is busy resetting/responding.
Freezing a wave?Hold minions near your tower to deny farm and set up ganks.
Item spikes?Key purchases (mythic/legendaries) that shift fight outcomes.
Roam timing?After pushing your wave or on enemy recall/death.
Objective bounties?Comeback mechanic rewarding the trailing team for taking objectives.
Best way to climb?One‑trick a champion, review replays, and focus on CS, vision, and macro.
